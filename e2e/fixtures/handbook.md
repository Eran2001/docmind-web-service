# Acme Handbook (test fixture)

## Time off

Every full-time employee gets 25 days of paid time off per year. Unused days up to 5 carry over to the next year.

## Remote work

Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week. Core hours are 10:00 to 15:00.

## Expenses

Submit expense claims within 30 days of the purchase. Receipts are required for any single expense above $25.
